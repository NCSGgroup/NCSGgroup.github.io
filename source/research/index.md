# Research

The NCSG Group focuses on numerical computation, satellite gravity, and geodetic data analysis.

## Satellite Gravity and GRACE/GRACE-FO

We study time-variable gravity fields using satellite gravity missions such as GRACE and GRACE-FO. Our research includes gravity field recovery, signal separation, leakage correction, and applications to Earth system mass transport.

Keywords: GRACE, GRACE-FO, time-variable gravity, spherical harmonics, mascon.

## Numerical Methods for Inverse Problems

We develop numerical methods for large-scale geodetic inverse problems, including regularization, iterative solvers, uncertainty quantification, and computational optimization.

Keywords: inverse problems, regularization, numerical linear algebra, optimization.

## Earth System Mass Transport

We use satellite gravity and complementary observations to study terrestrial water storage, ice mass change, sea-level change, and other mass redistribution processes.

Keywords: terrestrial water storage, ice sheets, hydrology, sea level, Earth system.

## Open Science and Reproducible Research

We promote open-source tools, reproducible workflows, and public data products through GitHub, Zenodo, and related open science platforms.
