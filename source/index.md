The NCSG Group focuses on numerical computation, satellite gravity, geodesy, and Earth system mass transport. We develop computational methods and satellite gravity techniques to investigate mass redistribution in the Earth system.

## Software

<div class="ncsg-project-grid ncsg-project-grid--list">

  <a class="ncsg-project-card" href="https://github.com/NCSGgroup/PyHawk">
    <div class="ncsg-project-card-header">

      <img src="../img/projects/logo_pyhawk.webp" alt="Satellite gravity project">

  </a>

  <a class="ncsg-project-card" href="/software/sagea/">
    <div class="ncsg-project-card-header">
      <h3>SaGEA</h3>
      <img src="../img/projects/logo_sagea.webp" alt="GRACE data processing project">
    </div>
    <div class="ncsg-project-card-body">
      <p>
        SaGEA is a Python toolbox for post-processing and error assessment of satellite gravity products.
      </p>
    </div>
  </a>

  <a class="ncsg-project-card" href="https://github.com/NCSGgroup/SaGEA-fluid">
    <div class="ncsg-project-card-header">
      <h3>SaGEA-Fluid</h3>
      <img src="../img/projects/logo_sagea_fluid.webp" alt="Numerical computation project">
    </div>
    <div class="ncsg-project-card-body">
      <p>
        SAGEA-fluid extends SaGEA to include self-attraction and loading effects, geocenter motion, and Earth rotation effects.
      </p>
    </div>
  </a>

  <a class="ncsg-project-card" href="https://github.com/NCSGgroup/PyGLDA">

    </div>
